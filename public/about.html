Why NutriGlance Was Created

Like many people striving to eat healthier and stay conscious of their nutritional intake, I constantly found conventional calorie counter apps frustrating:

  • Manual search fatigue: Typing in every individual ingredient, brand, and gram count turned eating into an accounting session.
  • Cluttered experiences: Many popular apps are filled with invasive ads, bloated paywalls, and slow interfaces.
  • Lack of real assistance: Knowing you have 400 calories left is useless unless you know what nutritious meal to cook next.

NutriGlance was built to solve this exact problem: take a quick photo or write a simple sentence, let AI vision recognize the meal and estimate calories and macros in seconds, and have a personalized AI Chef guide your nutrition goals.

3

Core Features & Architecture

NutriGlance combines modern engineering and performant AI inference:

AI Meal Scanner

Snap or drag & drop meal photos to auto-detect food items, estimate portion weights, and log calories, protein, carbs, and fats instantly.

AI Chef Assistant

Provides tailored recipe ideas and smart suggestions based on ingredients you have on hand and your remaining daily macro budget.

Progress & Hydration

Real-time daily calorie target rings, macro breakdown distribution, water tracking cups, and historical progress logs.

PWA & Cloud Sync

Installable as a Progressive Web App on Android, iOS, and desktop with offline support, Firebase authentication, and Firestore cloud synchronization.
