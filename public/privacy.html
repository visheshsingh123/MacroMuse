Privacy & Data Security

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. This policy explains how NutriGlance collects, protects, processes, and respects your meal scans, health targets, and personal information.

Effective Date: June 1, 2026 Last Updated: October 2026
1

Overview & Core Principles

NutriGlance ("we", "our", or "us") provides an AI-powered personal dietitian and nutrition logging service available via our web and mobile applications. We believe your dietary choices, meal photos, and biometrics belong strictly to you.

Our core privacy commitments:

  • We never sell or rent your personal, dietary, or biometric data to third-party advertisers or brokers.
  • Meal images are processed exclusively for nutritional analysis and recipe generation.
  • You retain complete control: you can inspect, modify, or permanently delete your meal history at any time.
2

Information We Collect

To provide nutritional insights, macro calculations, and personalized meal recommendations, we collect the following types of information:

Profile & Biometric Targets

Optional user profile details such as age, gender, height, current weight, daily target calories, and macro breakdown (protein, carbs, fat) used solely to calibrate your nutrition goals.

Meal Images & Food Logs

Photos of food, handwritten meal notes, text descriptions (e.g. "2 rotis with dal"), meal timestamps, estimated macro values, and daily water hydration counts you record.

Authentication Credentials

If you register an account, we securely manage your email address, display name, and Firebase User ID (UID), or public Google account profile via Google OAuth. Passwords are never stored on our servers.

Technical & Device Information

Browser type, operating system, and local display preferences (such as light or dark theme) to optimize visual presentation and offline PWA functionality.

3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to deliver and refine your dietitian experience:

  • Real-Time Meal Scanning: Analyzing uploaded food images through AI vision models to detect dishes and estimate calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats.
  • AI Chef Personalization: Formulating customized recipe ideas, ingredient suggestions, and nutritional alternatives based on your remaining daily macros.
  • Progress & Habit Tracking: Aggregating your daily intake, calorie burn ratios, and hydration streaks over weekly and monthly trends.
  • Cross-Device Synchronization: Syncing your meal timeline seamlessly across your browser, tablet, and mobile PWA via encrypted cloud database.
4

AI Processing & Third-Party Service Providers

NutriGlance integrates select, industry-standard infrastructure partners to process meal scans and authenticate users:

AI Inference (Groq & Vision APIs)

When you scan a meal or ask the AI Chef for recipes, visual or textual prompts are securely processed by high-speed inference endpoints. Image payloads are ephemeral: they are analyzed to extract food composition and nutritional data, not repurposed to train public models without your explicit consent.

Authentication & Storage (Google Firebase)

Account authentication and user records are managed via Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, protected by granular Firestore Security Rules ensuring users can only read and write their own data.

Hosting & Deployment (Vercel)

Our web infrastructure is hosted on Vercel's global edge network with automated HTTPS encryption, DDOS protection, and high-availability caching.

5

Data Storage, Local Caching & Security

We implement rigorous administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to maintain your data integrity:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your device, our serverless APIs, and AI models use modern TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Offline Local Storage: NutriGlance caches your current day's meals locally using browser localStorage so you can review meals even with intermittent connectivity.
  • Least-Privilege Database Access: Firestore rules enforce authentication checks so that no user can query or modify another user's dietary logs.
6

Your Rights & Data Control

You have full authority over your data. At any time, you can:

Delete Log Entries Remove any individual meal entry directly from your dashboard or meals history tab.
Update Targets Re-adjust your height, weight, activity levels, or target calorie thresholds in settings.
Purge Account Request complete deletion of your Firebase profile and associated food logs permanently.
7

Cookies & Local Storage

NutriGlance does not use invasive third-party tracking or advertising cookies. We strictly utilize necessary client-side storage technologies (localStorage and Service Worker cache) to:

  • Remember your light / dark mode color scheme preference.
  • Store offline session tokens so you remain signed in.
  • Cache nutritional meal history for rapid dashboard rendering.
8

Children's Privacy

NutriGlance is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or the applicable minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal or nutritional data from children under 13 without verified parental consent. If we become aware that such information has been logged, we promptly take steps to delete it.

9

Updates to This Policy

As we release new AI capabilities, nutritional calculators, or integrations, we may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant updates will be highlighted on this page along with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of NutriGlance after changes indicates your acknowledgement of the updated terms.
