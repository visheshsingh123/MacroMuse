GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might be wondering.

How do I activate Pro?

Choose Activate Pro, sign in, and enter your activation code. New activations unlock lifetime Pro on your account. A valid code can be used again, including after cancelling Pro. Paid checkout is not available yet.

Does Pro expire?

The new one-time Pro plan has no expiry or recurring charges. Earlier time-limited activations keep their existing expiry, shown in Settings. You can give up Pro access from Account & data; this does not issue a refund. You can activate Pro again with a valid code.

How much does Pro cost?

Pro is US$5 or ₹500, paid once for lifetime access. There is no monthly or yearly subscription.

What happens when I reach a Free limit?

The Free tier allows 3 meal logs and 5 AI Chef replies per day. Once you reach either allowance, you’ll be prompted to upgrade or return when that daily allowance resets. Your existing meals remain accessible.

What does unlimited AI Chef mean?

Pro has no daily cap on AI Chef replies. Short-term rate limits may apply to prevent misuse and keep conversations available for everyone.

Can I replace my saved recipe on Free?

Yes. The Free tier has one saved-recipe slot. Remove the saved recipe to make room for another, or choose Pro to keep an unlimited collection.

Is progress tracking only for Pro?

No. Both plans include progress tracking. Pro adds PDF exports so you can download a copy of your progress.
