GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you might be wondering.

Can I buy Pro today?

Not yet. Pro is launching soon at ₹200/month or ₹1,500/year. You can keep using Free while we prepare paid upgrades.

How much do I save with yearly Pro?

Monthly Pro is ₹200/month, or ₹2,400 across 12 months. Yearly Pro costs ₹1,500, saving you ₹900 (37.5%). That works out to ₹125/month, paid as one yearly amount.

What happens when I reach a Free limit?

The planned Free tier allows 3 meal logs and 5 AI Chef replies per day. Once you reach either allowance, you’ll be prompted to upgrade or return when that daily allowance resets. Your existing meals remain accessible.

What does unlimited AI Chef mean?

Pro will have no daily cap on AI Chef replies. Short-term rate limits may apply to prevent misuse and keep conversations available for everyone.

Can I replace my saved recipe on Free?

Yes. The planned Free tier has one saved-recipe slot. Remove the saved recipe to make room for another, or choose Pro to keep an unlimited collection.

Is progress tracking only for Pro?

No. Both plans include progress tracking. Pro adds PDF exports so you can download a copy of your progress.
