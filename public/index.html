A little more about us
How does photo meal tracking work?+

Upload a meal photo or write a description. NutriGlance estimates the calories, protein, carbs, fat, and portion details. Review the result, then choose to log it.

How accurate are the nutrition estimates?+

AI estimates can vary with ingredients, cooking methods, and portion sizes. A clear photo and a few details help. Review your entries and use package information when it is available.

What can I do on the Free plan?+

Free includes 3 meal logs and 5 AI Chef replies per day, plus calorie, macro, water, and progress tracking. Daily allowances reset at 00:00 UTC. Saving recipes and PDF exports require Pro.

What comes with NutriGlance Pro?+

Pro includes unlimited meal logs, AI Chef replies, and saved recipes, plus PDF progress exports. It is US$5 or ₹500 once for lifetime access. Activate with a code on the pricing page; the activation form does not take a payment.

Can I use NutriGlance on my phone?+

Yes. Use it in your mobile browser, install the web app on supported devices, or get the Android APK from Downloads. AI requests and cloud saves need an internet connection.
