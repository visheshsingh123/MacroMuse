FAQ

AI calorie tracker, answered

What is an AI calorie tracker?

An AI calorie tracker uses image recognition to identify the food in a photo and estimate its nutrition automatically. Instead of manually searching a food database and logging portions, you take a photo and the app calculates calories, protein, carbs and fibre for you.

How accurate is NutriGlance's meal scanning?

NutriGlance identifies each item on your plate and cross-references portion size and ingredients to estimate calories and macros. As with any photo-based estimate, accuracy improves with clear, well-lit photos, and you can always fine-tune an entry after it's logged.

Is NutriGlance free to use?

You can start tracking meals with NutriGlance for free — snap a photo, log macros, and see your daily progress without setting up a manual food diary.

How is an AI calorie tracker different from MyFitnessPal-style apps?

Traditional calorie tracker apps rely on you searching a food database and entering portions by hand. NutriGlance removes that step — a single photo replaces the search-and-log process, so tracking a meal takes seconds instead of minutes.
